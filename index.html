Common questions

How much does it cost?

Reviews start at £750 and the fee comes off any build that follows. Most projects cost a few thousand pounds, always at a fixed price agreed in writing first. Every proposal shows what the work should save, using your own figures, so you can see the payback before you commit.

Will we have to change all our software?

Usually not. We build around the systems your team already uses, so they keep working the way they know. If a new tool would genuinely do the job better, we’ll explain why.

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. You know your business and we handle the technology. Everything is explained in plain English, and your team is trained on whatever we set up.

How long does it take?

A review takes two to three weeks. Most builds take a few weeks and are delivered in stages, so you start seeing results early rather than waiting for one big launch.

What if my team doesn’t use it?

That’s the most common reason new systems fail, so we plan for it from the start. Your team helps shape what we build, we run it alongside your current way of working before switching over, and we check in after launch to fix anything getting in the way.

Is our data safe?

We work inside your own systems wherever possible, so your information stays under your control. Access is set up securely, kept to what the job needs, and removed when the work is finished.

What happens after it’s built?

The first 30 days of support are included. After that you choose: pay as and when you need help, buy a bank of hours at a lower rate, or, for systems we run for you, a simple monthly plan that covers running costs and care. No long contracts either way.

Where do you work?

We’re based near Weston-super-Mare and work with businesses across North Somerset, Bristol and the South West, where we can visit in person. We also work remotely with businesses anywhere in the UK.
